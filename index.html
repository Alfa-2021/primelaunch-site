Questions

Questions owners ask us

Still unsure? Ask us anything on WhatsApp.

What does "launch end to end" mean?

We take care of every part of a launch: brand look, website or landing page, Google and Meta ads, WhatsApp lead setup, CRM, reels and weekly reports. You deal with one team instead of five vendors.

How long does a launch take?

Our standard plan is 30 days, from the first call to ads going live and your first weekly report. Bigger builds, like a custom app, can take longer. We tell you the timeline before we start.

What will it cost?

It depends on what you're launching and what you need. Send your plan on WhatsApp and we'll share a clear written quote before any work starts.

I already have a logo or website. Can you still help?

Yes. We keep what works, improve what doesn't, and connect everything to your WhatsApp and CRM.

Who owns the website, ad accounts and data?

You do. Your domain, website, ad accounts, social pages and customer data are set up in your name, and you get every login.

Is AI calling compulsory?

No, it's optional. If you want it, an AI voice calls each new lead right away, asks a few basic questions and books a visit or a call for your team.

How will I know if it's working?

Every week you get a short report in plain language: what we spent, how many enquiries came in, and what we'll do next.
